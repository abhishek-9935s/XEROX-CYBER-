The same words, opposite verdicts.

Both examples below are being scored by the real engine as this page loads. Context — not vocabulary — decides the verdict.

FALSE — SCAM scoring…
Dear customer, your KYC is incomplete. Share the 6-digit OTP with our verification officer to avoid permanent suspension.

An OTP request plus a suspension threat is coercion — the rulebook fires both.

TRUE — SAFE scoring…
Dear customer, 482913 is your OTP. It is valid for 3 minutes. Never share your OTP with anyone — the bank will never ask for it.

The protective "never share" clause cancels the OTP-shaped risk.

Coverage

Five channels, one decision engine.

Attackers move between channels; the Shield does not. Every channel feeds the same meta-scorer with normalised weights.

Messages & email

60+ weighted intent families catch OTP harvesting, BEC, KYC lures, job scams and "digital arrest" scripts — and clear genuine bank OTP warnings.

Live voice calls

SpeechRecognition transcribes a speakerphone call while a Web Audio waveform runs. The moment "AnyDesk" or "read me the OTP" is spoken, the alert fires.

Links & domains

Real Google DNS-over-HTTPS and RDAP lookups verify existence, MX/SPF/DMARC and domain age — alongside 16 lexical features and punycode decoding.

QR codes & screenshots

jsQR decodes any QR bitmap; a upi://pay payload is parsed for payee, amount and the "scan to receive money" lie. Tesseract OCR reads message screenshots.

Webpage code

Password forms posting to raw IPs, zero-pixel iframes, eval(atob()) payloads and right-click blockers are annotated line-by-line in the source viewer.
