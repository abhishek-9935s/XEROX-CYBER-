The Training

Precision is a decision, not a metric.

A filter that blocks 100% of scams but eats 5% of normal mail gets switched off within a week. XEROX is trained on verified threat corpora and calibrated on a held-out cohort until false positives are near zero — then the threshold is exposed as a live control.

From raw corpus to calibrated barrier.

01

Collect & normalise

Merge corpora, strip HTML, decode MIME parts, canonicalise URLs, drop duplicates across sets.

02

Balance & split

Class-balance ham vs phish, stratify by source and era, hold out 100k for evaluation only.

03

Feature engineering

16 lexical/structural URL features; sender forensics; tokenisation for the transformer.

04

Train both heads

RandomForestClassifier (200 estimators) on tabular URL data; DistilBERT fine-tuned on intent labels.

05

Calibrate, don't just train

Choose the threshold that keeps FPR near zero while TPR stays above 93% — the slider above.

06

Ship to the browser

Export the transformer to ONNX, reimplement the forest's decision logic in JS, and run the whole ensemble client-side.
