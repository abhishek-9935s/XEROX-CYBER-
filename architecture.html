Three threat vectors. One calibrated decision.

Modern phishing is grammatically perfect, freshly registered and wrapped in obfuscated JavaScript. XEROX stops looking for mistakes and starts measuring manipulation.

Vector 1 — the message is not the problem, the intent is

Semantic intent NLP 60+ rule families

We do not score spelling or grammar. We score the psychological architecture of a message: urgency, authority, isolation, credential demand and financial extraction — then subtract protective language.

Hi Priya, I need this handled strictly confidential, before 3:00 PM today. The escrow wire of ₹48,50,000 must be initiated, and please keep this thread off the finance group. Reply to my alternate inbox — my corporate ID has sync issues.
BECUrgencyIsolationAuthorityFinancial
Same words, different intent

Context flips the verdict. A protective clause is treated as evidence for legitimacy, not as another keyword hit.

“Share the 6-digit OTP with our officer.” → credential theft
“Never share your OTP with anyone.” → protective
OTP-shaped digits alone never raise the score.
Vector 2 — URL structure & live telemetry

A flat “bad-domain list” is always one zero-day behind. Instead we measure the shape of the link and then ask the internet whether it is real.

  • 16 deterministic features
    Length, entropy, dots, hyphens, digits, @ symbols, IP hosts, punycode, risky TLDs, shorteners, query depth and Levenshtein brand distance.
  • RFC-3492 punycode decoding
    xn--pypal-4ve.com decodes to pаypal.com with a Cyrillic “а”, then normalises to the real brand.
  • Live RDAP age + DNS auth
    Domains younger than 30 days are flagged; missing MX/SPF/DMARC reduces sender trust.
Vector 3 — DOM & script inspection

The page you see is not always the code that runs. We parse the raw source and flag the machinery of theft.

  • Credential forms → raw IP / bot webhook
    Password fields whose action leaves the origin are the signature of a harvester.
  • Obfuscated payloads
    eval(atob(…)), String.fromCharCode, hex arrays and long base64 blobs.
  • Hidden frames & anti-inspection
    Zero-pixel iframes, oncontextmenu, F12 blockers and debugger traps are annotated line by line.
